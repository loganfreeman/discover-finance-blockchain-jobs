# Research Company Hiring Signal

Use this skill when a user wants to decide whether a company is worth applying to, how to approach the application, or what risks to understand before interviewing.

Company hiring data is time-sensitive. Use current web research when available. Prioritize official careers pages, investor/company announcements, reputable news, regulatory filings where relevant, engineering blogs, GitHub/org activity for open-source blockchain companies, and recent layoff or funding signals.

## Inputs

Useful inputs:

- Company name or URL.
- Target role family and seniority.
- Region or remote preference.
- User profile or resume summary.
- Specific concerns, such as layoffs, funding, visa support, compensation, or interview difficulty.

## Research Areas

Cover the areas that matter for the user's question:

- Company category and product.
- Current engineering openings.
- Hiring momentum.
- Business, funding, regulatory, or protocol health.
- Engineering stack and open-source activity if relevant.
- Fit for the user's target role.
- Application recommendation.
- Interview prep angles.

Use the shared `references/domain-taxonomy.md` categories when labeling the company.

## Recommendation Levels

Use one of:

- `Apply now`: strong current opening and acceptable risk.
- `Apply selectively`: some fit, but notable uncertainty or weak signal.
- `Monitor`: interesting company, but no current role or signal is weak.
- `Deprioritize`: poor fit, stale roles, or high risk for the user's stated goal.

## Output

Follow `templates/report.md`. Include source links and retrieval dates. Keep claims specific and distinguish confirmed facts from inference.

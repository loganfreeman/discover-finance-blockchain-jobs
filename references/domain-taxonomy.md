# Domain Taxonomy

Use this shared taxonomy when labeling companies, roles, and reports.

## Industry Categories

- `banking`: banks, payment rails, credit, lending, treasury, core banking.
- `fintech`: payments, cards, lending, payroll, accounting, consumer finance, B2B finance software.
- `trading`: market makers, exchanges, brokerages, execution venues, trading infrastructure.
- `quant-finance`: quant research, quant development, systematic trading, portfolio systems.
- `crypto-exchange`: centralized exchanges, custody, wallets, on/off ramps.
- `blockchain-infra`: L1/L2 protocol engineering, nodes, validators, data indexing, RPC, rollups.
- `defi`: lending, DEXs, derivatives, stablecoins, yield, protocol applications.
- `web3-security`: smart contract audits, protocol security, wallet security, custody security.
- `regtech-risk`: compliance, fraud, AML, risk scoring, financial crime systems.

## Signals

Positive hiring signals:

- Multiple current engineering roles matching the user's target.
- Roles appear on the company's own careers page.
- New roles were posted or refreshed recently.
- Hiring spans more than one team or geography.
- Recent funding, revenue growth, product expansion, or protocol activity supports the hiring story.

Risk signals:

- Roles only appear on third-party mirrors.
- Job links are stale or removed from the official careers page.
- Recent layoffs, hiring freezes, major regulatory actions, or severe token/business decline.
- Many openings with vague descriptions and no engineering specificity.
